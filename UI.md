## Student

Learning dashboard uses an operational cream/navy redesign: a dotted navy learning-journey hero, wide split next-class card, enrolled-course cards and four compact learning shortcuts. It deliberately contains no invented course imagery or decorative law/book graphics. The split `Next live class` panel appears directly below the hero (and therefore above progress on mobile), with countdown chips and tactile Join/Calendar buttons. Course progress is calculated from Admin-marked module completion; the course workspace uses a dense module accordion with a right-side access/progress/help summary, recordings, private files and module-specific live classes. Each course finishes with its own published course-materials and certificate section rather than sharing materials across courses. Meet and Google Calendar actions appear only for the learner's active batch and remain blocked for preview/unentitled users. The dashboard treats an ended class as non-joinable while preserving its configured calendar link. Locked resources remain visually previewed but are also blocked server-side/RLS.

`/admin/courses` manages batches, modules, published per-module class sessions, Google Meet/Calendar links, module files and separate course-level private materials. `/admin/students` focuses on learner-by-learner module completion; learners cannot self-mark progress.

## Admin

Combined command center follows the selected mock: grouped navy sidebar, KPI band, action queue, first-party funnel, website status, next class, recent purchase and integration health. Website Studio offers draft, preview and publish. Other operational modules share search, empty/loading and creation patterns.

`/admin/workshop` and `/admin/workshop/[id]` provide dedicated live workshop operations: aggregated multi-workshop overview cards with registration toggle and capacity; individual workshop command center with 6 live KPI cards; Meta Campaign Strategy attribution matrix ($6 General Taxpayer vs $4 Tax Professional); filterable participant management table with Bengali search, single-click attendance toggles, and UTF-8 BOM CSV export; detailed participant drawer with contact, UTM, and lifecycle audit; and modal settings to manage schedule, platform, and private Google Meet links.

`/admin/coupons` is the dedicated course-coupon control surface. It creates and edits one-course coupon rules, date windows, redemption limits and active state; browser-entered prices and discount totals are never trusted.

## Responsive/accessibility

- 390px and 1440px are core visual QA sizes; the practical return course is also checked at 430px, 768px and 1024px
- eBook also preserves the historical `max-w-6xl` layout and route-specific mobile sticky purchase CTA
- Mobile navigation/admin sidebar use accessible sheets
- Homepage tool controls become stacked touch-friendly panels, the checklist result follows its profile selector, and Tax Brief fields/interests stack without horizontal overflow
- The analytics privacy control is a 44px static footer button at the bottom-right of public and checkout footers; it never occupies the viewport or the lower-right WhatsApp support space
- Visible focus, medium-speed press states, reduced-motion support
- No horizontal overflow; Bengali wrapping and touch targets are tested
- Genuine empty states are explicit; production never shows local demo metrics
